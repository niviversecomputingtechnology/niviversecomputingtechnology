The vision

Lightweight, premium, and built around gesture — not menus.

The product brief sets a clear bar: replace traditional pop-ups and modals with fluid bottom sheets, keep every interaction spring-animated and one-handed, and support both light and dark themes without ever going pure black. Design language borrows the readability of WhatsApp and Telegram, the visual energy of Instagram and Discord — filtered through a calmer, more considered surface system.

Build status

What's built, what's mid-flight, what's ahead

An honest snapshot across every area of the app, verified directly against the current codebase.

Navigation shell & onboarding

In progress

Splash, login, and the four-tab home shell all navigate correctly with a fully custom tab bar. Authentication is UI-complete but not yet connected to a real sign-in service.

Conversations & groups list

Built

Chat and group list screens are live with filtering (All / Unread / Favorited / Archived), presence badges, unread counts, and multi-select — running on realistic sample data today.

Messaging experience

In progress

The conversation screen — bubbles, reactions, the long-press action sheet — is built to a high visual bar as a working demo. Next: connect it to real, per-conversation data and a send pipeline.

New chat & contacts

Planned

The entry point exists in navigation; the contact search, selection, and recents experience described in the brief is not yet built.

Attachments, voice & group creation

Planned

Data structures already model images, audio waveforms, files and video; the capture flows and bottom sheets that create them are next in line.

Notifications, settings & dark mode

Planned

Push notification wiring is scaffolded and ready to switch on. Settings and Saved are placeholder screens; dark theme has not been built yet.
